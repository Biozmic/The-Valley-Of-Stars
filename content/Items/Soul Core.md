#item 
Relics used in the War of the Gods as the beating hearts of animated constructs. Created through arcane soul binding and the soul-bearer's undying reverence to a chosen god.

As time passed and faded, so did the souls within. Soon becoming seemingly dormant, but still remained potent, and even eager to gather more magic in some cases, with many warnings surrounding them in favor of taking caution in their handling unless the soul within awakens.
