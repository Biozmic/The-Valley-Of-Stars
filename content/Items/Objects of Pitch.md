*unknown Rarity, wonderous item*
**Necromancy & Enchantment** (Assumedly) 

Solid black objects made by the [[Organizations/Thieves' Guild]] via an unknown ritual. Seemingly made from objects of personal value the victim once had. 

Creatures wearing these objects on their person appear to be **dominated** by an unknown force and are made to follow their command with no memory of it. If a victim has their mind restored to them while wearing this object, they're subjected to significant pain, and will be sure to die shortly.
