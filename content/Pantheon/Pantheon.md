In the world of Manna, the Gods work a little differently to most games. There exists a set hierarchy that has been in place for over a millennia. 

A long time ago, The 3 major Gods began to war on this plane, causing massive destruction wherever they decided was appropriate to do battle. After many years of this conflict, it came to a head and the 3 Elder gods: [[Pantheon/Order]], [[Pantheon/Balance]], [[Pantheon/Chaos]], all agreed to take a step back and influence the world indirectly in a way that would never cause as much damage as they just had. 

Upon this decision, each of the 3 Elder Gods made a pact to never step foot on this plane unless it was deemed absolutely necessary. To impact things more indirectly, they were allowed to choose 5 mortals each to ascend to endless life and un-aging body in the form of Demi-God status. Each of these 5 would be the stepping stone for mortals to worship and connect with in the way they would the gods. 

In addition to this, each Elder god, and each Demi-God were allowed to pick one "Chosen" to carry a shard of their will and power. It was ruled that no church was to turn away a follower of a God that doesn't fall under a certain category, and that forced union would build a bridge to allow the masses to see passed warring over "who was the right god" to follow. To this day, anyone can visit a local church, and using a method of divine communication imbued by the Gods, comment and speak with them at any time. It is said however, that a connection cannot be made if the Gods are walking around on the mortal plane, which for the ex-mortals, is not uncommon.
