A large population of dwarves made their home in the mountains of ***Mirror Rock***, rich with minerals and good stone. After the biggest gold rush in the history of [[Gazetteer/Irem/Irem]], Kirkwald exploded in population when everyone wanted to cash in on its bountiful resources. As such, Kirkwald now stands as the place to go to "strike it rich" for all races and ages. 

Kirkwald's **Lower District** houses many smaller businesses and civilian homes. It pales in comparison to its **Upper District**, home to its academic gem, the ***Riverwick University*** of Magical Studies. Many large businesses and mansions alike flaunt their wealth in their wares, chandeliers, and the gold blood of Mirror Rock speckled into its ornate walls.

## Points of Interest
### Riverwick University
The largest building in Kirkwald, second only to the King's castle, this place acts as one of the most prestigious schools in Irem. Mainly known for its in depth teachings of the Arcane, lead by professors who are masters in their respective schools of Magic

### Hunter's Guild
One of the many Guilds in Kirkwald. This one being dedicated to the posting and completion of tasks and quests across the region. Adventurers often gather here in a way to gain coin and to rent or lend items. Working their way up the qualification ranks with each quest, some eventually gaining a **Golden Status** within the Guild
