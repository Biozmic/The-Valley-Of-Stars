A small fishing village with its few hundred citizens making their living off the ***Iremese Coast***. Torma's many docks are frequented by fishing boats and arriving vessels from [[Gazetteer/Graad/Graad\|Graad]]. As for its main attractions, the sea air, seagull refuse, and a surprisingly predatory flea market that the natives warn against are its only notable features. A small church run by a sole tiefling priestess and a smattering of huts makes Torma a mere footnote in most people's travel logs during their time in [[Gazetteer/Irem/Irem]].
