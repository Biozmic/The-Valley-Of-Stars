An islet at the foot of [[Gazetteer/Irem/Kelna\|Kelna]] inhabited by those of elven heritage, the soil of **Nabol's Keep is permeated with natural magicks that the inhabitants use to sustain themselves.** Little is known about this isolated place as its ports are closed to all but a select few with magical and scholarly mettle. Those who are permitted entry, however, have claimed the island to be a spiritual oasis for those dedicate their lives to the secrets of the arcane arts.
