There is a rare nautical myth that when the moonlight glints the sea into a silvery sheen, and the ***constellation of Naphamer*** is at its brightest, a large ebon castle could be seen in the cradle of Megrum's waters. However, nobody has ever claimed to set foot on it, much less know its purpose. Ships that sail through the area are misdirected by thick fog, or breach their vessels to the rough coral reef. The castle's existence has been a long standing mystery, and there are a few curious people who have put a bounty on any information to help chart the waters to it. But would you put much stock into an old sailor's tale?
