On the Aerie's icy apex lies the ruins of a village, rumoured to have once housed a tribe of Leonin, a legendary race of lion-like humanoids. **Owing to the inhospitable climate and recent dragon sightings**, few have ever ventured up the mountain. 

Knowledge of the Leonin are limited to scant hearsay and scraps of myth - that their lineage was cursed with eternal life, without the ability to bear young. There is a tale of Dwarvish origin that states that any Leonin who kills one of their brethren will gain the strength of those they have felled. The Aerie has become somewhat of a mystical, fairy tale landmark that most tend to avoid due to the dangers of the climb, and the wildlife that reside there.
