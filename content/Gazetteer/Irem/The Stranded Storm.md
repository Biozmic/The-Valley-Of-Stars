Between South Megrum and Nabol's Keep is a patch of turbulent ocean, thick with storms, squalls and sleet. Commonly known amongst nautical travellers as "Umberlee's Despair" or "that shitstorm to the South", it is commonly advised by anyone to sail around the area, or not approach it at all if one could help it. Acts as a gateway to [[Gazetteer/Irem/The Valley of Stars]]
