In the crescent strip of [[Gazetteer/Irem/Irem]] lies Megrum, **divided politically and religiously into its North and South counterparts.** With the **North following the Order pantheon and the South worshipping the Chaos pantheon**, both are tied together in constant civil war. Given their occupation with each other and their self sufficient economy, neither of them concern themselves with western Irem all that often, and as such, visitors are rarely received in Megrum.
