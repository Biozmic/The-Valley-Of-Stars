Created by [[NPCs/Kirkwald/Professor Onglewip]] and four other dragons across the world, The Valley of Stars is a plane of existence crafted for the communion of chromatic and metallic dragons alike. Only accessible by trails granted by other dragonkin who know of the Valley's existence.
