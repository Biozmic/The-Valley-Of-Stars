The largest populated city whose bounties lie in fertile lands and abundant crops, Kelna offers her citizens a humble but comfortable life. The presence of nobility is scarce, but the little that reside here oversees the economic growth that keeps Kelna an important trade ally to her surrounding cities. Residing in the heart of [[Gazetteer/Irem/Irem]]'s crossroads, Kelna is a frequent stop for merchants and travellers, using her farmland and woodland to leverage her exports with other cities. However, there are an increasing amount of Kelnans moving to Kirkwald due to its recent influence.
