The lodestone settlement for orcs, goblins and similar foreign races, some settle in the arid lands of Bhagkul to retain their traditional values while being part of a larger and safer society. Bhagkul prides itself on its **militaristic strength and religious structure**. Exporting its stone and traditional orcish textiles in exchange for food and alcohol, Bhagkul remains a place of reasonable economic power, and has long lasting trade routes with both Kelna and Torma. Natives of [[Gazetteer/Irem/Irem]] are scarcely seen making a home in Bhagkul, as its current inhabitants are slightly aggressive to outsiders.
