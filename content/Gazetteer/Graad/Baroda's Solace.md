The largest city in [[Gazetteer/Graad/Graad]], both in size, castle, population, and reputation. Baroda's is a place of safety in [[Gazetteer/Graad/Graad]], ***ruled by a Dwarf named Gulfur*** who during a battle over 30 years ago fought and became the warlord of Baroda's after showing him unwavering strength in battle. Since his rise to power, the city he lords over has become more prosperous than one could dream while simultaneously being the most welcoming and peaceful. Make your way here for comfortable living, good food, lively taverns, and well paying work.
