One day, a large town was built at speeds never seen before, it was like it had just popped up overnight. Investigations showed that a whole civilization of imps, lesser devils, and other fiends decided to build a home here in [[Gazetteer/Graad/Graad]]. They were exiled from the hells after it became apparent it was being overburdened by fiends who were too nice to their prey and slaves, and went their separate ways. The town of devils has many unique things to offer, including strange and interesting cuisine, magic and enchantment straight from the hells, and even materials from other planes.
