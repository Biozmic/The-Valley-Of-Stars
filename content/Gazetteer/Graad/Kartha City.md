The second largest city on [[Gazetteer/Graad/Graad]], Kartha is down to its bones the most rich blood place you could ever hope to visit. Requiring a certain amount of coin to even be allowed access, this haven for the rich includes anything your money could desire, even orphans. Kartha has the ***largest library in [[Gazetteer/Graad/Graad]],*** including being host to make secret and locked away tomes never to see the light of day. Ran by an elected official, the concerns of your average citizen here is "how do I make more coin."
