This small village isn't trying to hide it, while being near to a port that connects this and multiple other continents, as well as Downmarket, this is a mercenaries guild and thieves' guild "pretending" to be a farmland village. Everyone knows and nobody really cares. This is your first stop in and out of [[Gazetteer/Graad/Graad]] depending on where you're coming from/going to so if you're looking for work, it's a good place to stop by. Just make sure you keep your coin pouch close.
