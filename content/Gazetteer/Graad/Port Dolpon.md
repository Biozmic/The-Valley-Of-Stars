Located on the waters, Port Dolpon is a hub for trading, renting ships, travel, and everything in between. Not much of a home state and ran by an ex pirate captain, this Port holds a great deal of respect in [[Gazetteer/Graad/Graad]] for evolving well into the local sphere of towns and cities. Taverns here are usually full to bursting, and many who seek adventure and coin will find work of varying levels of dirt and danger here.
