Devout followers of **[[Pantheon/Demi-Gods of Balance/Kelemvor\|Kelemvor]], Lord of the Dead, Judge of the Damned.** His followers form a brotherhood devoted to becoming the arbiters of death itself. To them, death is not a tragedy—it is a duty, an order that must be upheld. They do not seek to kill indiscriminately, but when judgment demands a life be taken, they are the blade that ensures it is done.

A no-nonsense, militant order, they have forged some of the deadliest warriors to serve their god’s will. They do not interfere with the domain of life, nor do they revel in slaughter. Their place is with the dead, and their purpose is to see death’s balance maintained.
